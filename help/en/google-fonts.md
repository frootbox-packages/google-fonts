**Fonts** copies fonts from Google Fonts to your website. Visitors then
load them from your own domain, so Google learns nothing about them and
no consent is needed.

## Import a font

Search the catalog by name or kind; without a name you get the most
used fonts. The server asks Google only when you search, at most once a
day. **Import** copies the files the languages of your website need,
e.g. Latin, and Arabic for an Arabic website. Tick **With italics** if
your texts need italic letters.

## Use it

Choose the font in the **Design** for headings or text. It works like
the fonts of the theme. If you add a language with another script
later, import the font again.

## Remove a font

**Remove** deletes the font and its files. While the design or its
draft uses it, choose another font there first.
